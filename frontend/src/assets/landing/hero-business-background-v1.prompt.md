## Prompt utilizado

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide photographic background for NexoMark, a small-business campaign management web application. Create a realistic professional editorial photograph, landscape wide 16:9, of a small independent business workspace preparing a product marketing campaign: a modest laptop viewed obliquely, unbranded cardboard product packaging, a folded neutral cotton garment, a simple notebook and a few printed photo contact sheets, arranged naturally on a matte light desk. Wide composition with the left half mostly clean soft neutral wall/desk negative space for readable website headline, objects toward the far right and lower edges. Navy blue and restrained muted turquoise accents on stationery, palette inspired by #14283F and #17B8AD with off-white neutrals. Soft natural window light, authentic tactile materials, restrained color grading, gently out-of-focus background, realistic photography not CGI. Avoid people, visible readable screen content, text, logos, watermarks, glowing lights, robotic symbols, network dots, futuristic holograms, exaggerated cinematic effects, coffee shop specificity. The image is a subtle background, not a UI mockup; do not place any interface or headline inside image.
